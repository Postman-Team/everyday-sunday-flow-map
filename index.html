Flow map · October 2026

Everyday Sunday × Postman

Every flow, every email, and the job each one does

The full map of the email flows we're building and rebuilding for Everyday Sunday. The monthly scope is 12 emails: 6 campaigns and 6 flow emails. The flows are built in 5 phases, paced to those 6 flow emails a month. Each flow has one goal. Each email has one focus and a short brief of what goes inside. All flows run in English and French.

Exit on success

The moment someone does what the flow is for, usually a purchase, they leave it. Abandonment flows are ranked Checkout, Cart, Browse, Site, and a shopper is only ever in the highest one.

Discounts come last

Offers appear only in the final email of high-intent flows and only where the offer ladder allows. Everything else sells on product, fit and proof.

Campaigns, not flows

New-collection launches and the December and April season switches run as planned campaigns. The Cross-Sell Flow handles category introductions customer by customer in between.
